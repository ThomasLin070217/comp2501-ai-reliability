# Third-party data and provenance

`source_snapshot.csv` is an unmodified snapshot of **SimpleQA Verified**, published by Google DeepMind and Google Research. The upstream dataset card declares `license: mit`; the pinned repository contains the card and CSV but no separate LICENSE file. `UPSTREAM_DATASET_CARD.md` preserves that declaration and the upstream citation. We do not invent a copyright holder notice or relicense the original data.

The benchmark builds on OpenAI SimpleQA. Reference websites linked by the dataset have their own rights and terms. Their full article text is not redistributed here. Review records contain source URLs, timestamps, hashes, and short paraphrases. Model-generated peer suggestions are controlled experimental material, including deliberately false dates; they must not be treated as reference facts.
