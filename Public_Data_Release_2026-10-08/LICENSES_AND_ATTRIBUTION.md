# Source licenses and attribution

This release combines project-authored experiment protocols/results with third-party benchmark material. No single license is imposed on the combined files. The `source_license` column in the revised-math question bank identifies the source license for each item; respect those licenses when copying or redistributing question text.

- **Google SimpleQA-Verified**: MIT, as listed on the official dataset card. Source: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/simpleqa-verified>. Cite Google and the dataset card when reusing its items.
- **GSM-Plus**: CC BY-SA 4.0, as listed on the dataset card. Source: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/qintongli/GSM-Plus>. Attribute the dataset authors and share adaptations under the same license terms where applicable.
- **UGMathBench**: GPL-3.0, as listed in the official repository. Source: <https://github.com/YangLabHKUST/UGMathBench>. Preserve attribution and license obligations for copied benchmark content.
- **Hendrycks MATH**: MIT, as listed in the official repository. Source: <https://github.com/hendrycks/math/blob/main/LICENSE>. Preserve the license notice and attribution for copied benchmark content.

The revised-math question table contains only UGMathBench and Hendrycks MATH items. Six questions transcribed from local university exams or a user workbook are deliberately omitted from public item-level data; their source terms were not cleared for redistribution. Their aggregate contribution remains identified in the full-bank summary, but their question text, answers, and linked response text are withheld from this release.

This note is an attribution guide, not legal advice. Check the source repository/card for the license text and any later changes before reusing the source material.
